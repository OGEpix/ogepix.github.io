Hermes Workspace

A private automation application for a single user.

What it is

Hermes Workspace is a privately operated automation agent. It has exactly one user: the owner of the Google Account that authorizes it. It is not distributed, published, or offered as a service to anyone else.

What it connects to

With the owner's explicit consent, the application reads and writes the owner's own Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Docs. Its purpose is to carry out routine tasks on those accounts on the owner's instruction, such as reading a spreadsheet range, appending rows, or listing calendar events.

How it works

The application runs on a private server controlled by the owner. It authenticates with Google using OAuth 2.0. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.

Privacy

No user data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. See the privacy policy.
